JINRO Melon Limited Edition: What It Tastes Like and How to Enjoy It

JINRO’s melon flavor arrived as a limited edition this year. Here’s what it tastes like and a few easy ways to enjoy it.

Fruit soju fans have a new flavor to look for: JINRO Melon.

Here’s what to know about JINRO Melon.

What Does JINRO Melon Taste Like?

JINRO Melon captures the sweet essence of melon in soju. The melon note is light and refreshingly sweet, with a clean finish.

If you’re new to fruit soju, it’s an easy flavor to start with. If you already know JINRO’s other fruit flavors, it’s a new one to add to your rotation.

A Limited Edition with a Black Label

JINRO Melon first arrived this spring as a limited edition in more than 20 countries, including the US, Japan, Vietnam, Australia and the UK. It was also JINRO’s seventh fruit flavor.

The bottle is easy to spot, with a matte black label that stands out next to the rest of the lineup.

Availability may vary by country and store.

Easy Ways to Enjoy JINRO Melon

JINRO Melon works for lots of different moments. Here are a few ideas:

With friends: Bring a bottle to your next get-together and pair it with shareable Korean dishes.

With a meal: Its light sweetness goes well with savory Korean dishes like fried chicken, kimbap or tteokbokki.

On a slow weekend: Chill a bottle, cut up some fresh melon and keep it simple.

Serve it well chilled for the cleanest melon flavor.

More Flavors in the JINRO Lineup

If you enjoy JINRO Melon, the JINRO fruit soju lineup also includes JINRO Grapefruit, Green Grape, Plum, Strawberry, Peach and Lemon.

With more flavors to choose from, it’s easier to find the one that fits your taste, or to pick a different one for each occasion.

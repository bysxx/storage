JINRO Sounds: Meet Shenchongchong, the Artist Turning JINRO into Music and Color

Here is another JINRO Sounds collaboration shaped by an artist's own creative style.

Chinese singer and music creator Shenchongchong (沈虫虫) has created a song inspired by JINRO and paired it with dopamine-inspired look changes.

Through music, color, and styling, he connects JINRO's flavors with different moods and the small moments that can brighten an ordinary day.

Who is Shenchongchong?

Born in 1994, Shenchongchong is a Chinese singer and music creator who has appeared in concerts and singing competition programs throughout his career.

His work brings music, performance, and visual storytelling together, making him a natural fit for a collaboration that goes beyond simply featuring JINRO on screen.

For the project, he wrote a song specifically for the collaboration and built the video concept around it. His soft, smooth voice gives the track its charm and makes the chorus easy to keep replaying.

From JINRO Flavors to Dopamine Colors

For this collaboration, Shenchongchong wrote a song centered on JINRO Peach and JINRO Green Grape, connecting their flavors with color, mood, and the small moments that can brighten an ordinary day.

The concept draws on the dopamine look-change trend, using shifts in color and styling to reflect the change in mood described in the lyrics. The video was then created around the track, translating its playful tone and flavor-inspired chorus into a visual concept.

To see how the concept comes together, here is a closer look at the lyrics behind the track:

In the chorus, the words describing each flavor are repeated three times. The expressions were carefully selected to work with the pronunciation, tones, and rhythm of the track, allowing the sweetness of Peach and the freshness of Green Grape to come through in a playful and memorable way.

Shenchongchong's smooth voice, original track, and playful take on JINRO make this one worth experiencing with the sound on. Give it a listen and see how he brings his own JINRO moment to life through color, styling, and movement.

Shenchongchong's JINRO Vibe

This collaboration follows the first JINRO Sounds project with Los Angeles-based artist Noa Bar.

While Noa Bar transformed JINRO bottles, glasses, and sounds from the table into part of the beat, Shenchongchong wrote a song with lyrics inspired by JINRO and created a video around the concept, bringing its flavors to life through colors, outfits, and changing moods.

The artists and styles may be different, but both interpreted JINRO through their own sound and creative perspective.

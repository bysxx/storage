JINRO Melon Is Here to Stay: Now Part of the Regular Lineup

The melon flavor that started as a limited edition is now a regular member of the JINRO fruit soju lineup.

Good news for fruit soju fans. JINRO Melon is moving from limited edition to the regular JINRO lineup.

That means the melon flavor that first arrived as a limited release is here to stay.

Here is what to know about JINRO Melon.

What Does JINRO Melon Taste Like?

JINRO Melon brings the sweet, fresh aroma of melon to soju. The melon note is light, clean and refreshingly sweet.

If you are new to fruit soju, it is an easy flavor to start with. If you already know JINRO’s other fruit flavors, it is a new one to add to your rotation.

From Limited Edition to Regular Lineup

JINRO Melon first launched as a limited edition. Now it is joining the regular lineup.

If you already love it, you no longer have to wonder when it will be gone. If you have not tried it yet, you now have more chances to find it.

Availability may vary by country and store.

Easy Ways to Enjoy JINRO Melon

JINRO Melon works for lots of different moments. Here are a few ideas:

With friends: Line up a few JINRO fruit flavors and see which one everyone likes best.

With a meal: Its light sweetness goes well with savory Korean dishes like fried chicken, kimbap or tteokbokki.

On a slow weekend: Chill a bottle, cut up some fresh melon and keep it simple.

Serve it well chilled for the cleanest melon flavor.

More Flavors to Choose From in the JINRO Lineup

JINRO Melon joins a fruit soju lineup that already includes JINRO Grapefruit, Green Grape, Plum, Strawberry, Peach and Lemon.

With more flavors to choose from, it is easier to find the one that fits your taste, or to pick a different one for each occasion.

Welcome JINRO Melon to the Lineup

If you have not tried JINRO Melon yet, now is a good time to pick one up. And if you are already a fan, you can keep enjoying it.

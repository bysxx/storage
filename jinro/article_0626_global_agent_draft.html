JINRO Global Agents Are Officially Live: Here's What They've Been Creating

The applications are closed, the agents have been selected, and their content is officially starting to appear across Japan, Vietnam, and the Philippines.

Now comes the fun part: seeing what they actually create.

JINRO Global Agent began as a call for fans and creators who already know how to make JINRO part of a dinner, a trip, or a night out. With the program now in full swing, those creators are putting their own spin on monthly missions and bringing them to life through their own cities, communities, and everyday routines.

A Quick Refresher on the Program

JINRO Global Agent is a mission-based supporter program centered around content creation.

Each month, selected agents create reels, videos, and social posts inspired by a new mission. The idea is simple: show how JINRO naturally fits into local culture, casual get-togethers, and the everyday moments people already love to share online.

The program launched across the Philippines, Vietnam, and Japan, bringing together socially active creators who are eligible to appear in alcohol-related content under local regulations.

Where the Agents Are Posting Right Now

The same bottle can tell a completely different story depending on where it appears.

Across Japan, Vietnam, and the Philippines, agents have been taking on locally tailored missions built around food, summer routines, nights out, and time with friends. The themes may overlap, but the way each market brings them to life feels distinctly its own.

Here is a closer look at what the agents have been creating.

In Japan, agents have been sharing their missions across Instagram and X, with many leaning toward polished, photo-led posts rather than Reels.

Their missions have taken JINRO outdoors for slow, relaxing moments, into casual gatherings with friends, and onto tables filled with different food pairings. Together, the posts show how naturally JINRO can fit into the quieter, everyday scenes people already enjoy sharing.

Every idol needs a mic. After Toad’s K-POP debut, here is the limited-edition gear fans will want to collect.

Step one of becoming a K-POP star? Getting your own signature mic.

Remember Toad’s big K-POP debut? New stage look, new spotlight, and one piece of gear you might have missed: a microphone shaped like a JINRO bottle.

It is part of the limited-edition K-POP Star Toad goods, and it might be the cutest mic you see this year.

A Microphone Shaped Like a JINRO Chamisul Bottle

Who says a mic has to look like every other stage mic? This one is shaped like a JINRO Chamisul Fresh bottle, with a round, spoon-shaped mic head on top.

If you have ever had dinner with Korean friends, you might get the joke. Picking up a spoon and singing into it like a mic is a classic move at the table. This mic turns that joke into real gear.

It also connects to your phone via Bluetooth, so it is not just for display. And it comes in two limited-edition designs, so collectors may want both.

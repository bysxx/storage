JINRO Toad’s K-POP Debut Gear: A Mic Shaped Like a Soju Bottle

Toad’s K-POP Star look came with limited-edition goods, including a mic shaped like a JINRO bottle.

Remember JINRO Toad’s big K-POP debut? This summer, Toad took the stage on limited-edition JINRO Green Grape labels, with a green and a pink K-POP Star look.

The new look came with limited-edition goods, too, including one piece of gear you might have missed: a microphone shaped like a JINRO bottle.

A Microphone Shaped Like a JINRO Bottle

Who says a mic has to look like every other stage mic? This one is shaped like a JINRO bottle.

If you’ve ever had dinner with Korean friends, you might get the joke. Picking up a spoon and singing into it like a mic is a classic move at the table. This mic turns that table joke into real gear.
